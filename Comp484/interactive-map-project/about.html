About this project

National Parks Maps

This website is a simple map collection focused on three national park areas in California and the surrounding Sierra Nevada and desert regions.

Yosemite National Park

Yosemite is known for granite cliffs, waterfalls, giant sequoias, deep valleys, and high-country wilderness. The included map focuses on campgrounds, roads, and access routes across the park.

Sequoia and Kings Canyon National Parks

These neighboring parks protect giant sequoia groves, deep canyons, high mountain passes, and extensive backcountry trails. The map shows the connected park landscape, facilities, and major routes.

Death Valley National Park

Death Valley contains salt flats, sand dunes, mountains, canyons, and some of the hottest and driest conditions in North America. Its map emphasizes the large road network and widely separated facilities.

Purpose of the site

The site organizes the maps into a homepage, individual map view, reference page, and general information page. The images are the main visual content, while the written sections provide context for each map.
